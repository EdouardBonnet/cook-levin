We prove that [binary-encoded CNF satisfiability is NP-complete](paper.html#m27) under polynomial many-one reductions. The proof implements a SAT verifier and a reduction from bounded computations through Boolean circuits and gate clauses. It includes correctness, termination, and polynomial running-time bounds for the machines. Machine classes come from [lax-434930](https://laxarchive.org/lax-434930/).
